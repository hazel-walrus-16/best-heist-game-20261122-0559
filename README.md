The Gilded Vault is a top-down midnight heist in a single museum hall: you are the thief, slipping past three patrolling guards with sweeping red vision cones and two wall cameras, to steal the Star of Midnight diamond from its vault and vanish through the green exit door. Move with WASD or arrows (or click to walk), hold Shift to sneak, spend a smoke bomb (Q) to blind a cone or toss a coin (F) to lure a guard away — but every second you are seen, the alarm meter climbs, and at 100% the heist is over.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
